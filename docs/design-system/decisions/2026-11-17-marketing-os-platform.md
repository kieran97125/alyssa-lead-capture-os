# Marketing OS product and customer identity

The product is Marketing OS, with SmartVolt publisher identity. Customer names remain workspace/brand dimensions. The existing operational surface keeps its established premium plum tokens and compact density; this release changes product labels, customer-account language, login and the platform control plane, not the campaign brands.

Platform pages reuse SystemButton/SystemSubmitButton, SystemDetails and semantic tokens. The shared submit control retains form-status pending feedback, disabled admission and keyboard behavior. Customer workspace owners retain their existing operational management rights; their account cards no longer call them system owners. Platform operators use a separate verified-email registry and route gate.

Stories cover shared submit controls and platform full, read-only, empty, created and unavailable states. A credential-free fixture provides desktop/mobile snapshots, overflow and accessibility checks. A shared-password customer session is explicitly denied platform access. Existing design-system and report-layout checks remain required.

Rollback: revert the application release and managed email templates; keep the additive workspace registry, existing customer bindings and operator audit. Customer owner authority still derives from its unchanged owner role. Do not drop customer data, reset identities, alter provider credentials or remove existing live campaign domains. Infrastructure names are reversible metadata on the same project IDs.

Full additional-customer admission remains closed pending complete data-plane isolation and operational acceptance; the platform screen displays this real state rather than claiming SaaS readiness from branding.
